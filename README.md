# Kijbev Modding

Farming Simulator 25 script mods, made by a husband and wife team. Website: [kijbev-modding.com](https://kijbev-modding.com)

Our mods are published on the official GIANTS ModHub and are free to download and use. A mod that cannot go on ModHub for licensing reasons is published here instead, with its source and its license.

## Live on ModHub

| Mod | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Unload Bale Wrap](https://www.farming-simulator.com/mod.php?mod_id=358448&title=fs2025) | Unloads the wrap film from a baler-wrapper or a standalone wrapper and refunds what is left of it, so you can switch wrap colors in the middle of a job. |
| [License Plate Randomizer](https://www.farming-simulator.com/mod.php?mod_id=358677&title=fs2025) | Gives every vehicle you buy a random license plate in the format of the map's country, across 21 European countries. |
| [Livestock Visibility](https://www.farming-simulator.com/mod.php?mod_id=364457&title=fs2025) | Lets you choose how many animals a barn displays, up to 500, with a separate limit for poultry. |
| [Mouse Click Actions](https://www.farming-simulator.com/mod.php?mod_id=366477&title=fs2025) | Lets you click doors, lights, the ignition and folding parts on vehicles with Interactive Control clickpoints. Hold R to freeze the view and free the cursor. |
| [To Pasture And Back](https://www.farming-simulator.com/mod.php?mod_id=364007&title=fs2025) | Takes a herd from a barn and drops it off on a field or pasture that suits the species, where it wanders until you call it home. |

Our mods include the Kijbev help panel (To Pasture And Back with its next update): Right Ctrl + F12 opens and closes a list of the key combinations of every active Kijbev mod.

## In development

| Mod | What it is |
|---|---|
| To Pasture And Back, RL Edition | To Pasture And Back for [Realistic Livestock RM](https://github.com/rittermod/FS25_RealisticLivestockRM), maintained by rittermod. It inherits the GPL from Realistic Livestock RM, so it will be published here, with its source. |
| Torque & Terrain | A physics overhaul: soft soil gives under a machine's weight, heavy machines compact the soil, and horsepower matters. |
| AgriWeather | Torque & Terrain's weather panel and soil moisture model as a separate mod, without the driving changes. |
